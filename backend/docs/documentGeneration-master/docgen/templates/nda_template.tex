\textbf{\color{t2ldark}2.\enspace CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION}

``Confidential Information'' means any data or information relating to the
business, operations, technology, financial information, trade secrets, or
affairs of the Disclosing Party designated as confidential or reasonably
understood to be confidential.

\vspace{5pt}
\textbf{\color{t2ldark}3.\enspace OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY}

The Receiving Party agrees to: (a)~hold all Confidential Information in strict
confidence; (b)~not disclose or reproduce it without prior written consent;
(c)~use it solely for the Purpose; (d)~notify the Disclosing Party of any
unauthorised disclosure immediately.

\vspace{5pt}
\textbf{\color{t2ldark}4.\enspace EXCLUSIONS}

These obligations do not apply to information that: (a)~becomes publicly known
through no breach of this Agreement; (b)~was rightfully known prior to
disclosure; (c)~is independently developed; or (d)~is required by law.

date, unless terminated earlier by mutual written consent.
